# Third-party notices

`src/engine/player.ts` adapts the movement and collision approach of the player controller from
**Agent Office** (https://github.com/AgentSystemLabs/agent-office). The look of the world also follows
Agent Office: its toon ramp, outline effect, lighting values and color palette, the plank floor painter
in `src/engine/office.ts`, the street, trees, neighbours, clouds, sky, weather and night lamplight in `src/engine/environment.ts`, the cigarette and smoke in `src/engine/smoke.ts`, and the basketball approach in `src/engine/ball.ts`. The files in `src/ao/` (the jukebox and arcade cabinet models, the BLOCKFALL game, the lo-fi tune synthesizer and the toon helpers) are copied from Agent Office with small changes.
